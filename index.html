About Me

I'm a second-year Mechatronics Systems Engineering student at SFU, actively working on two university design teams and a track record of competition results across Canada.

I'm drawn to problems that don't have easy answers. Whether it's deriving a control law from scratch, or designing a combustion chamber that won't melt itself — I want to understand things at a fundamental level, not just make them work.

My interest in rocketry comes from a fascination with space. There's something uniquely compelling about the engineering required to leave Earth — the physics are unforgiving, the margins are razor-thin, and the stakes don't get higher. That same pull toward the extreme end of what's possible is what draws me to theoretical physics: the idea that the universe has deep, elegant structure waiting to be uncovered.
