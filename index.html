About Me

I'm a mechatronics engineer with a background in applied physics, focused on designing intelligent systems that bridge computation and the physical world. My work spans embedded firmware, control theory, mechanical simulation, and signal processing.

I'm most at home when a problem requires thinking across domains — whether that's modelling rigid body dynamics, implementing a PID controller in C, or running FEM analysis on a custom actuator.

Currently open to robotics, hardware, and research roles where deep technical work is the job, not the exception.
